Bible Study

Leviticus

Holiness, sacrifice, priestly theology.
“... so that you can distinguish between the holy and the common, between the unclean and the clean.” (Leviticus 10:10)

The key to the whole book is chapter 10, verse 10: separate the holy from the common, and the clean from the unclean.

Five themes — blood, victory, glory, the throne of the King, triumph over the powers

Lesson 2

The Five Offerings and Drawing Near to God

Lev 1–7: burnt offering, grain offering, fellowship offering, sin offering, guilt offering — five doors for approaching God. (In preparation)

Lesson 3

The Priesthood, the Glory, and Unauthorized Fire

Lev 6–10: the ordination to holy office, the appearing of the glory, and the unauthorized fire of Nadab and Abihu. (In preparation)

Lesson 4

Clean and Unclean: Body and Food

Lev 11–15: the regulations on food, childbirth, skin disease, and discharges — how together they shape a life of distinction. (In preparation)

Lesson 5

The Day of Atonement: the Summit of Cleansing

Lev 16: the center of the book — the one day each year when the Most Holy Place is entered. (In preparation)

Lesson 6

Blood and the Holy Life

Lev 17–20: blood belongs on the altar; the Holiness Code carries the altar’s distinctions into everyday ethics. (In preparation)

Lesson 7

The Holiness of the Priests and the Feasts

Lev 21–24: the boundaries binding the priests themselves, and the times set apart within the year. (In preparation)

Lesson 8

Sabbath Year, Jubilee, and Covenant

Lev 25–27: the land’s rest, the return of inheritance, and the book’s blessings and curses (with course review). (In preparation)
