託付與使命 The Mandate and the Mission

講員：曾金發牧師（Rev. Edmund Chan）場合：2026 年 7 月第十屆世界華人福音會議

經文版本：《和合本》英文：講員原文

開場與禱告Opening & Prayer

司儀：歡迎曾金發牧師。
Emcee: Welcome, Rev. Edmund Chan (Zeng Jinfa Mushi).
各位晚安。能與你們同聚，是何等的喜樂。恭賀華福迎來五十週年——這是神恩典與信實何等奇妙、何等美好的里程碑。
Good evening. It is such a joy to be with you, and I congratulate CCCOWE on your 50th anniversary. What an incredible, fantastic milestone of God's grace and faithfulness.
今晚我要向各位講一件根本要緊的事。身為領袖，我們必須分辨什麼是根本的、什麼是次要的。請與我一同低頭禱告，求主賜福這段時間。
I want to speak to you tonight about something that is essential. And as leaders, we must distinguish between the essentials and the non-essentials. So would you bow with me in prayer as we ask the Lord to bless this time together.
永恆的神、天上的父，求祢開我們的眼睛，使我們看出祢話語中奇妙的真理；幫助我們不只作聽道的人，也作行道的人，好叫我們因此長進。奉耶穌奇妙的名感謝祢。阿們。
Eternal God and Heavenly Father, open our eyes to behold wonderful truth out of your word. And help us not just to be hearers of your word, but doers also, that we might grow thereby. We thank you in Jesus' fantastic name. Amen.

引言 · 快車道上的提醒Living in the Fast Lane

我最喜歡的故事之一，是一位第一次到美國的日本遊客。他從機場搭計程車前往旅館，看見美國街頭的日本車，非常興奮。
One of my favourite stories is of a Japanese tourist who went to the USA for the first time. He took a taxi from the airport to the hotel, and he was excited to see Japanese cars in the US.
他對司機說：「你看你看，Toyota，日本製造，好快！哦你看，Mitsubishi，日本製造，好快！」轉個彎他又說：「哦你看，Honda，日本製造，好快！」
He said to the taxi driver, "Look, look, look—Toyota, made in Japan, very fast. Oh, look, look—Mitsubishi, made in Japan, very fast." Turned round the corner and he said, "Oh, look—Honda, made in Japan, very fast."
到了旅館，他嚇了一跳，車資怎麼這麼高。他問司機：「車資怎麼這麼貴？」紐約的司機說：「計費表——日本製造，好快。」
By the time he reached the hotel, he was very shocked that the taxi fare was so high. And so he asked the taxi driver, "How come the taxi fare is so high?" And the New York taxi driver says, "Taxi meter—made in Japan, very fast."
忙碌使人忘記根本：我們活在快車道上，有時就因為忙碌，忘了那根本要緊的事。We live so much in the fast lane that sometimes we forget the essentials because of our busyness.
我們正處在極具策略性的時代，有著策略性的機會。是的，這是充滿挑戰的時代。但正是在這樣的時代，神為教會打開了奇妙的策略之窗。這是許多事都懸而未定的時代，神呼召我們與祂一同進入信心的偉大歷險——為祂的國度、為祂的旨意、為祂的榮耀。
We are living in very strategic times with strategic opportunities. Yes, they are challenging times. But there are times like these where there are wonderful strategic windows of opportunities that God is opening for the church. These are times where so much is up for grabs, and God calls us to a grand adventure of faith with Him—for His kingdom, for His purposes, for His glory.
但要承接神的旨意，我們必須回到根本。
But in order to embrace God's purposes, we must return to the essentials.

預備爭戰 · 看不見的根基Battle Ready: The Unseen Foundation

朋友，回到根本，就是預備好上戰場。因為在領導的位分上，我們身處屬靈爭戰的戰區。
You see, my friend, returning to the essential is about being battle ready, because we face a spiritual warfare zone in leadership.
教會不是遊輪，教會是戰艦，我們不可輕忽這場爭戰。The church is not a cruise ship. The church is a battleship. And we must not neglect the battle.
這場爭戰所對付的並非彼此，也並非屬血氣的人，而是為了人的靈魂，與黑暗的執政掌權者爭戰。屬靈的爭戰真實存在，我們必須守住正確的焦點。
The battle is not against one another. It's not against flesh and blood. It's against the principalities of darkness for the souls of men. There is a spiritual warfare, and we must keep our focus right.
聖經經文 · Scripture
以弗所書 6:12　因我們並不是與屬血氣的爭戰，乃是與那些執政的、掌權的、管轄這幽暗世界的，以及天空屬靈氣的惡魔爭戰。“The battle is not against flesh and blood. It's against the principalities of darkness for the souls of men.”
我要定睛於神，定睛於祂的國度，定睛於祂的呼召，定睛於使萬民作門徒的大使命。但要做到這一切，我們的根基必須穩固。根基的特點，就是根基是看不見的。
I'll focus upon God, I'll focus upon His kingdom, I'll focus upon His calling, I'll focus on the Great Commission to disciple the nations. But in order to do so, our foundation must be right. You see, the thing about foundation is that the foundation is unseen.
這正是內在生命如此重要的原因，也是為什麼培育內在生命是今日聖經門訓的關鍵議題。研究領袖跌倒的案例，你會發現核心都是裡面的妥協。
And that is why the inner life is so important. That is why cultivating the inner life is a crucial agenda in biblical discipleship today. Because if you study leadership derailment, you find at the heart of it is a compromise within.
最大的爭戰不在外面，最大的爭戰在我們裡面。The greatest battle is not outside. The greatest battle is within us.
因此今晚我要與各位分享「託付與使命」。使命重要，但使命必須被託付托住。託付先於使命。
And that is why tonight I want to share with you about the mandate and the mission. The mission is important, but the mission must be cradled by the mandate. The mandate precedes the mission.
我要與各位分享的，就是這種由內而外的生命：託付是根基，託付是那根本的事，託付就是我們與神同行、作耶穌的門徒。因此，從「由外而內」轉向「由內而外」的門訓，這關鍵的範式轉移，是完成耶穌交付大使命的要害。
What I want to do basically is to share with you this inside-out, this inner life where the mandate is the foundation, the mandate is the essential, the mandate is our walk with God and our discipleship unto Jesus. Therefore, this critical paradigm shift from outside-in to an inside-out discipleship is crucial in fulfilling the Great Commission Jesus has given to us.

王與四位妻子的故事The King with Four Wives

有一個很有啟發的故事，講到一位有四位妻子的王。王被告知得了重病，無人能醫，將要離世，他非常懼怕。他不想獨自走向死後的世界，就去找他極其寵愛、要什麼給什麼的第四位妻子。
One of the instructive stories I find is about a king who has four wives. The king was told that he had a great sickness and nobody could heal him. He was going to die, and he was very frightened. He didn't want to go to the afterlife alone, so he went to the fourth wife, whom he loved very much and had given her everything she wanted.
他對第四位妻子說：「我要去死後的世界了，你願意陪我去嗎？」第四位妻子說：「我愛你，也知道你很愛我，但我不能陪你去。」
He said to the fourth wife, "I'm going to the afterlife, would you accompany me there?" And the fourth wife said, "I love you, and I know you love me a lot, but I cannot accompany you to the afterlife."
他去找第三位妻子。她非常美麗，王常驕傲地在鄰國君王面前展示她。他說：「我要去死後的世界了，你願意陪我嗎？」第三位妻子說：「我不能陪你去。而且你死後，我要改嫁。」他非常憂傷。
He said to the third wife, who is very beautiful, and he was very proud to show the third wife to the neighboring kings and kingdoms, "I'm going to the afterlife, would you accompany me?" And the third wife said, "I cannot accompany you. In fact, when you die, I'm going to remarry." He was very sad.
他去找常伴左右的第二位妻子：「你願意陪我去死後的世界嗎？」第二位妻子說：「是的，我陪你走過人生四季，但死後的世界我不能陪你去。我最多能為你辦一場好的喪禮，好好送你一程。」
He went to his second wife, who was constantly with him, and said, "Would you accompany me to the afterlife?" The second wife said, "Yes, I've been with you through the seasons of life, but I cannot accompany you to the afterlife. The best I can do for you is to give you a good funeral and send-off."
王非常憂傷，因為這三位妻子都不願陪他。這時他聽見第一位妻子的聲音：「王啊，我願意陪你去死後的世界。」王滿心懊悔自責，因為他一直冷落這位妻子。他對她說：「這些年我都忽略了你，你卻是唯一願意陪我去的人。」
The king was very sad because none of these three wives would accompany him. And then he heard the voice of his first wife. And the first wife said, "King, I will accompany you to the afterlife." And the king was struck with regret and remorse, because he had neglected the first wife. And he said to the first wife, "All these years I've neglected you, but you're the only one who would be willing to accompany me to the afterlife."
某種意義上，我們都像這位有四位妻子的王。第四位妻子代表我們的身體：我們餵養它、順著它，但當我們離世，身體並不跟著我們去。第三位妻子代表我們的財物、我們的財富：我們死時帶不走財富，財富反要歸給別人；我們所擁有的，將被別人擁有——就像那位改嫁的妻子。第二位妻子代表家人：他們陪我們走過人生四季，卻不能陪我們進入死後的世界；我們死時，家人最多能為我們辦一場好的喪禮、好好送別。
All of us, in a way, are like the king with four wives. The fourth wife represents our body—we feed it, we give it what it wants, but when we go to the afterlife, the body doesn't accompany us. The third wife represents our possessions, our wealth—when we die, we cannot take our wealth with us; in fact, our wealth will be given to somebody else. Our possessions, somebody else will possess—it is like the wife remarrying. The second wife represents the family—they have been with us through the seasons of life, but they cannot accompany us to the afterlife. When we die, the best the family can do for us is give us a good funeral and a send-off.
被冷落的第一位妻子，代表的是靈魂——靈魂會跟著我們進入死後的世界。The first wife, the one who is neglected, represents the soul. The soul will accompany us to the afterlife.
這就是為什麼要培育裡面的生命——持守與主同行的託付，好讓祂的真理、祂的光、祂的旨意、祂的榮耀照亮我們，滋養我們的靈魂，加深我們的生命，在生命與事奉中打下穩固的根基。靈魂，這內在的生命，至關重要。
And that is why cultivating that life within—the mandate of walking with the Lord so that His truth, His light, His purposes, His glory shine upon us—nourishes our soul, deepens our life, builds a strong foundation in our life and our ministry. The soul, the inner life, is so crucial.

內在生命的五個層面Five Dimensions of the Inner Life

朋友們，我們談內在生命，但要怎樣界定內在生命？內在生命指的是什麼？我提出內在生命的五個重要層面。
My friends, we talk about the inner life, but how do we define the inner life? What is the inner life about? I have five significant dimensions of the inner life.
第一，是我們裡面的驅力（compulsions）。驅力有黑暗的驅力，也有光明的驅力。黑暗的驅力是癮癖；光明的驅力則是熱情。這些是推動我們的力量——或是捆綁我們的癮，或是我們在神的事上的熱望。
The first is our inner compulsions. There are dark compulsions and compulsions of the light. Dark compulsions are addictions, whereas when you come to the compulsions of the light—the passion—these are the things that drive us: either our addiction, or our aspiration in the things of God.
第二，是確信（conviction）。確信與信念不同。
The second is the conviction. Conviction is different from beliefs.
信念是我們所持守的；確信是持守我們的。神呼召我們建立深刻的確信。Beliefs are what we hold on to. Conviction is what holds on to us. And God is calling us to build deep convictions.
第三，是良心（conscience），關乎我們怎樣生活。良心可能麻木。所以在主面前保持敏銳的良心、常常及時清帳、帶著溫柔、悔改與感恩而行，何等重要——這關乎良心，也關乎第四樣：人生的指南針（compass），由內而外的定向。裡面的安穩正是從這指南針而來，而那份安穩，就是第五樣：我們內在的沉穩（composure）。
The third in the inner life is our conscience—in how we live. And the conscience can be seared. That is why to have a sensitive conscience before the Lord, to keep a short account before the Lord, to walk with that sense of meekness and repentance and gratitude, is so important—in both the conscience and the fourth, the compass in life. That inner restedness comes out of the compass, and that restedness is our inner composure.
我們的驅力（熱情）、我們的確信、我們的良心、我們人生由內而外的指南針，以及我們的安穩——這五者構成內在生命。
So our compulsion—our passion—our conviction, our conscience, our compass in life from the inside out, and our restedness: they form the inner life.
今晚我要與各位看四位聖經人物。他們教導我們何為託付，好叫我們能完成使命——與神同行的託付，使我們的內在生命得著更新、受教、在主裡剛強。
There are four Bible characters I want to share with you tonight that teach us about the mandate so that we can fulfill the mission—the mandate to walk with God so that our inner life is being refreshed, tutored, and strengthened in the Lord.

一 · 大衛：神看內心David — The LORD Looks at the Heart

聖經經文 · Scripture
撒母耳記上 16:7　耶和華卻對撒母耳說：「不要看他的外貌和他身材高大，我不揀選他。因為，耶和華不像人看人：人是看外貌；耶和華是看內心。」“But the LORD said to Samuel, 'Do not look on his appearance or on the height of his stature, because I have rejected him. For the LORD sees not as man sees: man looks on the outward appearance, but the LORD looks on the heart.'”
請問：以利押、亞比拿達、沙瑪，這三位舊約人物有什麼共同點？他們都是耶西的兒子。連同其餘四個兒子，七人一同列隊經過撒母耳面前。敬虔的撒母耳奉神差遣造訪他們的家。先知撒母耳帶著使命來到耶西的家——大衛的家——要膏立一位王。
Now here's the question: what do Eliab, Abinadab, and Shammah have in common? What do these three Old Testament characters have in common? They are sons of Jesse. And together with the rest of the other four sons, seven of them were paraded before Samuel. The godly Samuel came to their house for a visit because he was sent by God. So the prophet Samuel was on a mission as he came to the house of Jesse, the house of David, to anoint a king.
但兒子們一個一個走過，神都說：這個我沒有揀選，這個也沒有。最後撒母耳問耶西：你還有別的兒子嗎？有——在田野牧羊的少年大衛。他們把他召進來，撒母耳認出：這就是神所呼召的人。
But as each of the sons went through, God said, "No, not this one, not this one." Until finally Samuel asked Jesse, "Do you have another son?" Yes—a young shepherd out in the field called David. They called him in, and Samuel recognized: this is the man called of God.
請留意，用神的眼光衡量生命極其重要。聖經說：人看外貌，神看內心。連撒母耳自己，在為全地最高的職分——君王，最重要的位分——進行這場面對面的遴選時，也在看外貌。他心想：這人看起來不錯，有才幹，有王者之相。但神說：我看的並非外貌，我看內心。
Now, notice: it is very important that we evaluate life from God's perspective. The Bible says man looks at the appearance; God looks at the heart. Even Samuel himself, when he was doing this face-to-face interview for the highest office in the land, the most important job—the king—he was looking at the appearance. He was saying, "This person looks good, looks talented, looks kingly." But God says, "No, I don't look at the appearance; I look at the heart."
神的這句回答——「我看內心」——對我們的門徒生命極具啟發。因為我們今日活在一個迷戀「看得見的成功」的世界。我們常把活動與成就誤當作真實；我們錯誤地用觸及的廣度、進展的速度、成果的能見度、事工的規模來衡量成功。
The answer given by God—"I look at the heart"—is very instructive for us in our discipleship. Because we live in a world today that is obsessed with the visibility of success. We often mistake activity and accomplishment for authenticity. We misguidedly measure success by the breadth of our reach, the speed of our progress, the visibility of our results, and the size of our ministry.
把問題問對：該問的不只是「我能為神做更多嗎？」更要問：「我願意與神更深嗎？」We have to get our question right. The question is not merely, "Can I do more for God?" Rather, the question is, "Will I go deeper with God?"
是的，使命勢在必行。是的，儘管以熱情和委身服事神。但託付——與主的同行——必須托住我們的使命。所以身為領袖導師，我常懇切地對領袖們說：我們必須從講台走向路徑。
Yes, the mission is imperative. Yes, by all means, serve God with passion and with commitment. But the mandate, the walk with the Lord, must cradle our mission. That is why I lovingly tell leaders, as a leadership mentor, that we must move from the platform to the pathways.
今晚我站在講台上對你們說話。講台是發揮影響力的地方，是事奉的地方。我沒有要各位忽視講台——這是主所賜的講台。今晚我來，並非因為我必須說點什麼；我來，是因為我相信神給了我當說的話。講台自有其位。但我們必須學習從講台的聚光燈下，走進門徒的路徑——與耶穌同行、與神同行的路徑。因為講台關乎影響力，路徑卻界定並決定我們的誠信。
Tonight, I'm speaking to you from a platform. A platform is a place of influence. A platform is a place of ministry. I'm not saying ignore the platform—it is a platform given of the Lord. Tonight I come to you not because I have to say something; I come to you because I believe God has given me something to say. There's a platform. But we must learn to move from the limelight of the platform into the pathway of discipleship, of walking with Jesus, of walking with God. Because while the platform is for influence, the pathways define and determine our integrity.
怎樣行走、怎樣生活，是有分別的：許多領袖關心的是怎樣領導；神關心的是我們怎樣生活。How we walk makes a difference. How we live our lives makes a difference. For many leaders, we are concerned with how we lead. God is concerned with how we live.
回到大衛的故事，聖經說神看內心——而內心，是在與神獨處的時光中得著滋養的。
And so when we come to the story of David, the Bible says God sees the heart. And the heart is nourished in that time alone with God.
我作主任牧師二十五年，卸任交棒至今約十四年。在交棒之前，有許多「最後一次」：最後一次長執會、最後一次同工會、最後一次禱告會、最後一篇講道、最後一場歡送晚宴……許多的最後一次。但在我卸下新加坡聖約播道會主任牧師的職分、轉入帶領「全球刻意門訓教會聯盟」的事奉之前，我所做最重要的「最後一件事」，是帶著兩位新任主任牧師上山禱告。
I've been a senior pastor for 25 years. I've transited in leadership for about 14 years now. Just before I transited in leadership, there were many last things: a last board meeting, last staff meeting, last prayer meeting, last sermon to preach, last farewell dinner, etc. Many last things. But the most significant last thing I did as senior pastor of Covenant Evangelical Free Church, before I transited into the ministry of leading the Global Alliance of Intentional Disciple-Making Churches, was to bring the two new senior pastors up to the mountain to pray.
因為作主任牧師期間，每年十月我會離開辦公室一個月，上山尋求主。記得第一次上山，我說：「主啊，不遇見祢，我就不下山。」主真的顯現了。
Because as a senior pastor, every October I take a month away from the office, and I go up to the mountain, and I seek the Lord. And I remember the first time I went up to the mountain, I said, "Lord, I will not come down until I've encountered You." And the Lord showed up.
我原以為遇見神之後，我會滿有能力、滿有恩膏地下山。哪知主顯現時，祂光照我的罪與虧欠、我的驕傲與自以為是。我還記得，在耶利米書的十章經文裡，祂指出我二十項具體的罪。我一項一項地悔改，哭到最後對主說：「主啊，停，停——我的心承受不住了。」
I thought when I encountered God, I would come out strong, I would come out anointed. Oh, when the Lord showed up, He convicted me of my sins and my inadequacies, my pride and my presumptuousness. And I still remember, in ten chapters of Jeremiah, He convicted me of twenty specific sins. I repented of them one at a time. I cried, until I said to the Lord up on the mountain, "Lord, stop, stop—my heart cannot take it anymore."
我帶著被光照的心走下山，卻滿懷感恩。感恩的是：走門徒的路徑，不必倚靠自己的力量、自己的聰明、自己的智慧；感恩的是：主耶穌幫助我、堅固我。因為沒有祂的引領和堅固，單憑自己的力量與智慧，門徒的路徑根本走不下去。
And I walked off the mountain convicted, but grateful. Grateful that I do not have to depend on my own strength or my own intelligence or my own wisdom to try to walk the pathway of discipleship. Grateful that the Lord Jesus helps me and strengthens me. Because without His leading and His strengthening, it is impossible to walk the pathway of discipleship—not in our own strength, not in our own wisdom.
比事奉的高度更重要的，是根基的深度。主不被外貌或講台打動——祂看的是內心。Indeed, more important than the height of our ministry is the depth of our foundation. Because the Lord is not impressed by appearances, or the platform—He looks at the heart.

二 · 摩西：當行的道與當做的事Moses — The Way to Walk and the Work to Do

聖經經文 · Scripture
出埃及記 18:20　又要將律例和法度教訓他們，指示他們當行的道、當做的事。“Then teach them the statutes and the laws, and make known to them the way in which they are to walk and the work they are to do.”
幾年前，我摯愛的朋友 Luis Bush（路易士・布希）打電話給我說：「Edmund，能來對我『10/40 之窗』運動的領袖們講一堂信息嗎？」我說：「Luis，我愛你，也敬佩你的事工，但我去不了，我正夾在兩趟行程之間。」我旅行很多，那段時間實在不夠——在他特會期間，我還有下一趟行程要出發。
Some years ago, my beloved friend Luis Bush called me up and said, "Edmund, can you come to speak to my leaders in the 10/40 Window movement?" I said, "Luis, I love you and admire your ministry, but I can't. I'm in between trips." I travel a lot, but this was a season where there was not enough time for me to come to his conference, because there was a next trip I needed to go on in the midst of it.
他說：「Edmund，特會在峇里島，就在新加坡隔壁。飛進來，向領袖們講一堂大會信息，再飛出去。」我說：「Luis，我做不來。飛進飛出，那是名人做的事，我不行。」Luis 說：「我們就是盼望你這樣做。飛到峇里島講完信息，當天就能回新加坡，接著預備你下一趟的行程與特會。」我為此禱告，後來也去了——因為主給我負擔，要我分享的正是這節經文。
He said, "Edmund, the conference is in Bali—it's a neighbor to Singapore. Just fly in, share one plenary message with the leaders, and fly out." I said, "Luis, I can't do that. Fly in, fly out—only celebrities do that; I can't." And Luis said, "We'd love for you to do that. Just fly in, share the message in Bali, then fly back to Singapore the same day, and be ready for your next travel and conference." I prayed about it, and I did—because the Lord gave me a burden to share from this particular verse.
這節經文指示他們「當行的道、當做的事」。當行的道，是託付；當做的事，是使命。
This particular verse showed them the way they are to walk and the work they are to do. The way they are to walk is the mandate. The work they are to do is the mission.
我當時對他們分享的，基本上就是今晚要對各位說的：我們誤解了出埃及記十八章。許多基督教領導學著作引用出埃及記十八章時，常說其中的關鍵原則是「授權分工」——根據的是 21、22 節：揀選有才能的人，讓他們審判，把事分派給他們。但我要說：我們誤讀了葉忒羅給摩西的勸告。
And so what I shared with them is basically what I want to tell you tonight: we have misinterpreted Exodus chapter 18. In a lot of Christian literature on leadership, when Exodus 18 is quoted, it is often said that the key principle is leadership delegation—found in verses 21 and 22: you select able men and you let them judge; you delegate to them. But I'm here to tell you that we have misinterpreted Jethro's advice and counsel to Moses.
重點並不在授權。葉忒羅對摩西說的是：摩西，聽著，你這樣做不好。你自己必疲憊，百姓也必疲憊。聽我的，我給你一個勸告，願神賜你智慧——守住你的焦點。你要專注教導他們兩件事：當行的道、當做的事。其餘一切使你分心的，都分派出去：揀選有才能的人，讓他們去帶領。你要授權，但你要守住焦點。
The focus is not on delegation. What Jethro was telling Moses is: "Listen, Moses, what you do is not good. You will wear yourself out; you will wear the people out. Listen to me, I want to give you advice, and the Lord give you wisdom. Keep your focus. This is what you are to focus on: you are to teach them two things—the way they are to walk and the work they are to do. Everything else that distracts you from this focus, you delegate out. You select able men and let them lead. You delegate. But you keep your focus."
焦點在第 20 節。第 21、22 節的揀選與授權，是為了幫助摩西守住第 20 節的焦點。那焦點是什麼？教導、指示、勸勉他們兩件事：當行的道、當做的事——託付與使命。而且次序要緊：託付先於使命。換句話說，焦點不單在當做的事；首先，是當行的道。因為人怎樣行走，決定他們的心、他們的忠誠、他們對神的效忠。
You see, the focus is in verse 20. Verses 21 and 22—select men and delegate—are to help Moses keep the focus in verse 20. So we ask: what's the focus? You teach them, you instruct them, you exhort them on two things: the way they are to walk and the work they are to do—the mandate and the mission—and the order is important. The mandate precedes the mission. In other words, the focus is not just on the work they are to do; first, it's the way they are to walk. Because the way they walk determines their heart, their loyalty, and their allegiance to God.
這是重要的領導原則：我們自己先在所行的道與所做的工上守住焦點，才能教導別人當行的道與當做的事。
It's an important leadership principle: we keep our focus in the way we walk and the work we do, so that we can teach others the way they are to walk and the work they are to do.
託付 The Mandate
  • 我們的行走 our walk
  • 我們的真實 our authenticity
  • 我們的所是 our being
  • 我們的品格 our character
使命 The Mission
  • 我們的工作 our work
  • 我們的成就 our accomplishment
  • 我們的所做 our doing
  • 我們的才幹（輔助使命） our competence assists it
天國的首要呼召：先於「拓展國度」的，是「住在基督裡」——住在基督裡，國度才得以拓展。拓展國度是使命；住在基督裡是託付。I often say in discipleship: the first call of the kingdom is not to advance the kingdom. The first call of the kingdom is to abide in Christ, so that the kingdom might advance. Advancing the kingdom is the mission. Abiding in Christ is the mandate.

三 · 瑪挪亞：生活方式在先，使命在後Manoah — Manner of Life before Mission

聖經經文 · Scripture
士師記 13:12　瑪挪亞說：「願你的話應驗！我們當怎樣待這孩子，他後來當怎樣呢？」“Now when your words come true, what is to be the child's manner of life, and what is his mission?”※ 講員引用的英譯將此句譯作「這孩子的生活方式當如何？他的使命是什麼？」——「生活方式」在前，「使命」在後；本講道的論點正建立於此譯法的次序上。
瑪挪亞是參孫的父親。耶和華的使者向他顯現，啟示參孫將長大成為神大能的僕人。請留意父親瑪挪亞所問的問題。他沒有問使者：「你說的是真的嗎？」他說的是「願你的話應驗」——既然你說的必要成真，我有兩個問題：這孩子的生活方式當如何？他的使命是什麼？
Manoah was the father of Samson, and an angel of the LORD came and gave a revelation that Samson would grow up to be a mighty servant of God. And notice the question Manoah the father asked. He did not ask the angel of the LORD, "Is what you say true?" Rather, he says, "When your words come true"—in other words, since what you say is true, I have two questions: what is the child's manner of life, and what is his mission?
次序再次要緊：生活方式在先，託付在先，行走在先——然後才是使命。Again, the order is important. The manner of life comes first. The mandate comes first. The walk comes first. And then the mission.
華福歡慶五十週年、邁向未來之際，你們要專注於託付與使命。但在專注使命的同時，別忘了託付——要把它放在首位。因為託付、我們的行走，必須先於使命。
As CCCOWE celebrates your 50th anniversary and you move forward into the future, you have to focus on the mandate and the mission. But in focusing on the mission, do not forget the mandate. Put it first. Because the mandate, our walk, must precede the mission.
當人把行走放在工作之前、託付放在使命之前、真實放在成就之前，他就把優先次序擺正了。
When he comes to order the walk before the work, the mandate before the mission, the authenticity before the accomplishment, he is setting his priorities aright.
神所定的優先次序，帶來神所賜的權柄。偏離那優先次序，屬靈的權柄就被妥協了。There is a God-given priority that gives us our God-given authority. We shift from that priority, we compromise on our spiritual authority.
職位未必受損——你仍是部門主管、牧師、宣教士；事工也未必受損——你仍能講道、仍能帶領、仍能服事。但當屬靈的優先次序被妥協，屬靈的權柄就被妥協了；當我們顛倒神所定「託付先於使命」的次序，屬靈的權柄就受了虧損。
Our position might not be compromised—you are still the head of your department, or the pastor, or the missionary. Your ministry is not compromised—you can still preach, you can still lead, you can still minister. But the spiritual authority is compromised when our spiritual priorities are compromised. Our spiritual authority is compromised when we reverse the order that God intended—for the mandate to precede the mission.

四 · 保羅：凡事蒙祂喜悅Paul — To Please Him in All Respects

聖經經文 · Scripture
歌羅西書 1:10　好叫你們行事為人對得起主，凡事蒙他喜悅，在一切善事上結果子，漸漸地多知道神。“So that you may walk in a manner worthy of the Lord, to please Him in all respects, bearing fruit in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God.”
凡事蒙祂喜悅——這是託付；在一切善工上結果子——這是使命。To please Him in every respect—that's the mandate. To bear fruit in every good work—that's the mission.
今晚，展望未來之際，我們必須回到基本，回到根基，回到那根本的事——與主同行的託付。這一點不容妥協。
Tonight we must come back to the basics as we look at the future. We must come back to the foundation, the essential, the mandate in our walk with the Lord. It cannot be compromised.

普世教會的妥協 · 靈魂裡的兩套系統The Global Church's Compromise: Two Operating Systems

我要讓各位看見，教會——不單華人教會，更是普世教會：亞洲、非洲、拉丁美洲、西方——如何因忽略託付而妥協了我們的行走。
I want to show you how we as a church—not just the Chinese church, but the global church: Asia, Africa, Latin America, the West—how the global church has compromised our walk because we neglected the mandate.
我們根基的起點是神的話。聖經是我們的根基，是神在祂啟示中所賜下的權柄。我們要照著這權柄重新對齊、重新確立神在我們生命中的權柄：順服祂的話，承認聖經的真理，並且照著活。改變生命的關鍵不在於累積聖經知識，而在於把真理實行出來。
You see, our starting point in our foundation is the Word of God. The Bible is our foundation, the Word of God. It is the authority that God has given in His revelation—the authority by which we have to once again be realigned to and re-establish God's authority in our life: obeying His Word, affirming the truth of the Scriptures, and living by it. It is not the acquisition of knowledge of the Bible; it's the application of the truth that changes our lives.
真理本身不改變生命；被實行出來的真理，才改變生命。我說過許多次，今晚再說一次。I've said it many, many times, so tonight I'm going to say it again: Truth doesn't change lives. It is truth applied that changes lives.
聖經經文 · Scripture
馬太福音 7:24　所以，凡聽見我這話就去行的，好比一個聰明人，把房子蓋在磐石上。“Anyone who hears these sayings of mine and does them—practices them, applies them—I will liken him to a wise man who built his house upon the rock.”
那是耶穌的話：實行、遵行、應用。問題來了：我們有神的話，認識祂的真理，也愛祂的真理；但在人生的變數、壓力與試煉中，我們發覺實行神的話很難。為什麼？因為那時我們心想：好難、好苦。而且有一場爭戰，欺哄我們的心思偏離神的真理與能力。
That's the words of Jesus: practice, do, apply. Now, here's the problem. We have the Word of God. We know His truth. We love His truth. But in the contingencies of life, the pressures and the trials of life, we find it difficult to practice God's Word. Why? Because now we are thinking, "Oh, it's so hard, it's so difficult." And there is a warfare that deceives our mind away from God's truth and power.
於是我們憑著遠離神話語的感覺作選擇；選擇成了習慣，把我們從神的話引偏——因為此刻我們的思想、習慣、選擇都不再扎根於聖經。我們信了撒但的謊言：實行神的話太難，要求太高，門訓太苦，順服神的話根本不可能。帶著這謊言，我們沒有在神的能力裡於祂話語上成長、成熟——那本需要時間——反倒緩緩地、不知不覺地漂離了神的話。
And so, out of our own feelings, distant from the Word of God, we make our choices. Our choices become habits, and it leads us astray from the Word of God—because now our thinking, our habits, our choices are not grounded in the Scriptures. We believe the deception of Satan that says it is hard to practice the Word of God, it is demanding, discipleship is so difficult, obeying God's Word is impossible. With this deception, rather than growing and maturing in the Word of God in His power—which takes time—we drift very slowly from God's Word.
不久，我們的靈魂裡便寫進了另一套程式：另一種觀點、另一種確信、另一種屬世的價值。問題就在這裡：我們的靈魂裡如今有兩套源代碼、兩個權柄。
Very soon we have another thing codified within our soul—another perspective, another conviction, another worldliness. Now, here's the problem: there are now two source codes in our soul, two authorities.
靈魂無法同時運行兩套作業系統：正如手機或電腦無法同時跑兩個作業系統，人的靈魂也無法同時運行「神的話語」與「世界的價值觀」這兩套系統。Just like your handphone or your computer system cannot operate on two operating systems, the human soul cannot operate on two systems: the Word of God, and the worldview of the values of the world.
那怎麼辦？在普世教會、在基督徒中間，有三種回應。第一種是悔改。我們來到主面前說：主啊，對不起，我們偏了。我們要回轉，藉著祢的話重新確立祢在我們生命中的權柄。我們悔改，並且說：主啊，跟隨祢很難，因為肉體的本性不容許；但主啊，我們願與基督同釘十字架——如今活著的不再是我們，是基督在我們裡面活著。這正是我們所要的。我們悔改。這是第一種回應。
So what do we do? In the global church, amongst Christians, there are three responses. The first response is repentance. We come before the Lord and say, "Lord, we are sorry. We missed. We want to come back to re-establish your authority in our lives through the Word of God." We repent. And we say, "Lord, it's difficult to follow you, because our carnal nature doesn't allow for it. But Lord, we want to be crucified with Christ—nevertheless we live, yet not us; Christ lives in us. That's what we want." We repent. That's the first response.
第二種回應：有些基督徒退後，離開神，不再回轉。如果只有這兩種回應，事情倒還清楚：你或者屬基督和祂的國，或者不屬。今日普世教會門訓的難處在於出現了第三種選項。它本不該成為選項，我們卻取了它。
Second response: there are Christians who backslide, and they walk away from God because they don't turn back. Now, if there were only two responses, it would be quite clear: either you are for Christ and His kingdom, or you are not. The problem with discipleship today in the global church is that there's a third option. It shouldn't be an option, but we have taken the third option.
教會裡許多基督徒不退後，也不悔改——因為太難了。他們怎麼做？他們對神只在口頭上敷衍。他們來聚會，唱詩敬拜，參加查經和小組，也能講道、能分享；但在日常的行事為人裡——在職場、在家中，尤其在無人看見的私密世界裡——寶座上坐的是自己，而非基督。他們只在嘴唇上尊主為大。
Many Christians in the church—they don't backslide, they don't repent, because it's too hard. What do they do? They pay lip service to God. They come to church, they sing and worship God, they go to Bible study and cell group, they can preach and share. But in their daily walk—in the office space, in their home, and most importantly, in their private world—their self is enthroned, not Christ. They give lip service to Christ.
這就是今日普世教會的問題：我們妥協了託付。我們錯過了神那極大的呼召——與祂同行，經歷祂的恩典與大能，以致帶著感恩、喜樂、降服而行，重新確立祂在我們生命中的權柄，因為我們深知祂配得這一切。
That's the problem in the global church today: we have compromised our mandate. We have missed that tremendous call of God to walk with Him and to see His grace and His power, so that we walk with a sense of gratitude, a sense of joy, a sense of submission, where we re-establish His authority in our lives—because we know He is worth it all.
託付必須先於使命——否則，我們的使命早已被妥協。The mandate must precede the mission—otherwise we have already compromised our mission.

耶穌所問最重要的問題"Do You Love Me More Than These?"

聖經經文 · Scripture
約翰福音 21:15　耶穌對西門彼得說：「約翰的兒子西門，你愛我比這些更深嗎？」“Do you love me more than these?”
因此，耶穌問過最重要的問題，是祂對彼得的那一問——也藉著對彼得的問話，問到我們身上：「你愛我比這些更深嗎？」你愛我勝過使命嗎？勝過事工嗎？勝過成就嗎？勝過這一切嗎？對耶穌的愛、與祂的同行——這託付必須先於使命；否則，使命必大受虧損。
That is why the most important question Jesus ever asked is a question to Peter—and through his question to Peter, to us: "Do you love me more than these? Do you love me more than the mission? Do you love me more than the ministry? Do you love me more than the accomplishment? Do you love me more than this?" The mandate—the love for Jesus and the walk with Him—must precede the mission. Otherwise, the mission is severely compromised.
徹底的門徒生命是可能的：合乎聖經的、徹底的門徒生命，在基督裡是可能的——這可能，並非因為我們剛強，而是因為基督信實。I'm here tonight to tell you that radical discipleship, biblical discipleship, is possible in Christ—not because we are strong, but because Christ is faithful. It is possible.

來自非洲的見證A Testimony from Africa

最後，我以一個來自非洲的見證作結。幾年前，我有幸以師徒方式帶領 Isaias Acerbo，而 Isaias 又帶領了一位主教，名叫 Aloysius Arbo。Arbo 主教於一九八八年八月信主，一九九五年蒙召進入全職事奉。以下是他親口的見證，經他同意後分享。
I want to close with a testimony from Africa. Some years ago, I had the privilege of mentoring Isaias Acerbo, and Isaias mentored a bishop called Aloysius Arbo. Bishop Aloysius was converted in August 1988, and in 1995 he was called into full-time ministry. This is his testimony as he shared it, and I share this with permission.
他說：「門訓讓我看見自己裡面從不自知的軟弱：驕傲、容易被激怒、不體恤妻子、婚姻中缺乏良好的溝通、讀聖經只是為了別人（預備服事），還有許多其他。」二〇一九年，Arbo 主教加入了 Isaias 主領的門訓訓練。
He said: "Weaknesses I never knew were there—pride, being easily provoked, not understanding my wife, lack of good communication in my marriage, reading my Bible for others, as well as many other things." In 2019, Bishop Arbo joined the discipleship training which Isaias was conducting.
他接著說：「不久之後，身邊的人開始見證我正成為一個新的人。」教區隨後宣告二〇二〇至二〇三〇年為「門訓十年」。「儘管我迫切盼望身邊每個人都經歷這樣的翻轉，『大處著想、小處著手、深處扎根』這原則卻仍鮮明地在我心中。二〇一九年十一月，我只從四位資深牧師開始。」之後加入十二位帶職傳道，再擴及會吏長、傳道人與他們的妻子，組成門訓小組——每組五對以上的教牧夫婦。
And then he said this: "It was not long before people around me began testifying to the new person I was becoming." And the diocese declared 2020 to 2030 as a decade of discipleship. "As urgent as I wanted everyone around me to experience the transformation, the principle 'think big, start small, build deep' was fresh in my mind. I started with only four senior pastors in November 2019." Then twelve lay pastors were brought in, and it included the archdeacons and the lay pastors and their wives in discipleship groups—small groups of more than five clergy couples each.
當他們把託付放在首位，教會開始在屬靈深度上被更新。全國教會注意到這個教區的轉化，其他主教問他：你是怎麼做的？於是他從七位主教開始，最後有五個小組、每組七位主教——他在奈及利亞聖公會師徒帶領著三十五位主教。
The church began to be transformed in its spiritual depth as they put the mandate first. And the national church began to notice this church and its transformation, and other bishops asked him, "How do you do it?" So he started with seven bishops, and eventually he had five cohorts of seven bishops—five groups, which means 35 bishops that he was mentoring in the Church of Nigeria.
他在見證的結尾這樣說：「最令我喜樂的，並非這運動擴展得多廣，而是運動中人們生命明顯的改變：勝過罪的見證、婚姻與家庭得醫治、事奉與生活態度的正向轉變、合一、相愛與彼此體諒——不只在我自己的教區，更遍及奈及利亞聖公會全部一百六十五個教區。」
He ended his testimony by saying this: "Above all, my greatest joy about this movement is not how wide it is growing, but the noticeable change of life demonstrated by those in the movement—the testimonies of victory over sin, healing in marriages and homes, positive change in approach to ministry and life issues, unity, love and understanding—and not just in my own diocese, but in all the 165 dioceses in the Church of Nigeria."
當託付先於使命，門訓就結出果子。Discipleship works when the mandate precedes the mission.

結語 · 教會要產出什麼？Closing — What Is the Church to Produce?

我以一個簡單的問題作結。鞋廠生產鞋子，紙廠生產紙張，玻璃廠生產玻璃——教會要產出什麼？教會要產出門徒。而問題不在於你有沒有造就門徒、你的教會有沒有造就門徒；根本的問題在於：我們正在造出什麼樣的門徒？
I want to close with this simple question. A shoe factory produces shoes. A paper factory produces paper. A glass factory produces glass. What is the church to produce? The church is to produce disciples. And the question is not, "Are you producing disciples?" or "Is your church producing disciples?" The essential question is: what kind of disciples are we producing?
今晚的信息：弟兄姊妹，我們要造就的門徒，是生命中託付先於使命的門徒——他們與神的同行，托住他們為神所做的工。Tonight, my message is: my brothers and sisters, we must produce the kind of disciples in whose lives the mandate precedes the mission. Their walk with God undergirds their work for God.
請與我一同低頭禱告。永恆的神、天上的父，我們的委身界定我們的門徒身分。求祢領我們回到這託付——重新與祢同行、重新確立祢在我們生命中的權柄——這根本的事。親愛的神，求祢在此幫助我們。
Would you bow with me and pray? Eternal God and Heavenly Father, our devotion defines our discipleship. So lead us back to this mandate, the essential of walking with you afresh and re-establishing your authority in our lives. Help us in this, dear God.
朋友們，我相信每當聖經被翻開，神都在召喚我們作出回應。今晚也一樣。今晚，神呼召我們回應：向祂重新委身，重新確立祂在我們生命中的權柄，把託付放在使命之前。
My friends, I believe each time the pages of Scripture are opened, God summons us to a response. Tonight is no different. Tonight, God is calling us to a response: to make a fresh commitment unto Him, to re-establish His authority in our lives, to put the mandate before our mission.
你若說：「是的，主，我聽見祢的話。我從這四位聖經人物身上學到，把行走放在工作之前何等重要，好讓我的工作被我的行走托住。」這若是你向主的心願與回應，此刻可否邀請你與我一同站立？
And if you say, "Yes, Lord, I hear Your Word. I learned from these four Bible characters how important it is to put our walk before our work, so that our work is cradled by our walk"—if this is your desire and your response to the Lord, can I invite you right now to stand with me?
無論你在哪裡，你說：主啊，我要回到託付，我要高舉託付。我要能行祢吩咐摩西所行的：按著次序，教導他們當行的道、當做的事。今晚，當我們思想華人教會的未來——未來，始於把託付放在使命之前。今晚，我們就以祢僕人的身分如此行。這若是你的回應，請此刻與我一同站立。
Wherever you are, you say, "Lord, I want to return to the mandate. I want to lift up the mandate. I want to be able to do what you told Moses to do: teach them the way they are to walk and the work they are to do—in that order." And tonight, as we think about the future of the Chinese church: the future comes when we put the mandate before the mission. And tonight, we will do it as your servants. If this is your response, you stand with me right now.
永恆的神、天上的父，我為每一位站立的禱告，也包括我自己。我們軟弱，但祢剛強。求祢幫助我們把託付放在使命之前：我與祢的同行，托住並加力於我為祢所做的工。奉耶穌奇妙的名感謝祢。阿們。願神賜福你們。
Eternal God and Heavenly Father, I pray for these ones standing, including myself. We are weak, but you are strong. Help us to put the mandate before the mission: our walk with you to cradle and empower our work for you. We thank you in Jesus' fantastic name. Amen. God bless you.
